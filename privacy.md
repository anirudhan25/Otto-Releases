# Otto privacy policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Otto is a desktop research workspace made by Anirudhan Vijay. This page says what Otto stores, where, and who can see it.

## What stays on your computer

- Your PDFs, your library, reading progress, highlights, notes, Pages and tasks are stored on your computer.
- Otto works fully without an account. Signed out, nothing below about accounts or sharing applies.
- Voice input is transcribed on your computer. Audio is never uploaded or kept.
- Sign-in tokens for connected services are kept in the macOS Keychain or Windows Credential Manager.

## What is stored online, and when

- **Account.** If you sign in, Otto stores your email address, name, profile picture and any profile links you add.
- **Shared projects.** If you choose to share a project, its paper details, highlights, comments, Pages and tasks are stored online so your collaborators can see them. PDFs are not uploaded and are never sent to collaborators. Pages you mark private are not shared.
- **People search.** Other signed-in users can find you by name or email to invite you. Personal email addresses are shown partly hidden.
- **Feedback.** A report you send with the bug button includes your description and basic app and system details. It does not include your PDFs, their text, or file locations.

Online data is held by Supabase, our database provider.

## The assistant

When you ask Otto (the assistant) to do something using a cloud model, the text needed for that request, which can include passages from the paper you are working on, is sent to OpenAI or Anthropic to produce the answer. We record how much each account uses, not what was asked. If you choose a model running on your own computer, nothing is sent.

## Google

- **Sign in with Google** gives Otto your name, email address and profile picture, used only to create and show your account.
- **Google Docs and Drive** are optional. Otto asks only for access to files it creates or that you pick yourself. It cannot see the rest of your Drive.
- **PDF backup to your own Google Drive** is off unless you switch it on. Copies go to your Drive only.

Otto's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google data is not used for advertising, not sold, and not used to train AI models.

## Other services you may connect

Zotero, Mendeley, Zoom and Microsoft Teams are optional. Otto uses them only to do what you ask: import or send references, or create and open a call link. Otto does not record or receive call audio or video.

Searching for papers sends your search words to public scholarly sources such as OpenAlex, Semantic Scholar, Crossref and PubMed.

## What we do not do

We do not sell your data, show adverts, or track you across other apps or sites. We never ask for your university password.

## Deleting your data

Removing Otto and its data folder deletes everything stored on your computer. To delete your account and everything stored online, email the address below. You can disconnect Google at any time in Otto's settings or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
